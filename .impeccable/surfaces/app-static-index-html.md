# Alex chat screen

Scope: the single working screen (profile, conversation, evidence). Mode: Operate.
Audience: hackathon judges watching a projected live demo. Job: see a profile go in, rules and live data get applied, and a cautious answer come out.
Constraints: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step; desktop/projector first, phone usable; WCAG AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every answer is issued like a banknote: engraved, serial-numbered and backed by visible evidence. It refuses the category default of a chat bubble with a collapsible "tool calls" drawer.

OWN-WORLD: Currency paper ground (#ecede4) with intaglio face-green (#1f3a2e) owning the header band and primary controls. Back-green (#3f6b52) for labels, near-black ink, and a reserved evidence ink (#8a5a00) used only for citations and tool calls. Red/blue fibre hues are for state only. Guilloché line work, Cinzel caps for the wordmark only, Source Serif for answers, Public Sans for UI, tabular figures everywhere.

STORY: The judge sees the connected sources, picks a persona, asks a question, watches a rosette engrave while Alex works, then reads an answer whose every rule and data point traces to the evidence rail.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 72px green guilloché band (wordmark left, live source status center, session serial right). A 3-column grid: profile 340 | conversation | evidence 380. The composer is pinned at the bottom of the conversation, and is the primary action.

FORM: Intaglio Note, candidate 4 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 3070fa58. Raises: reserved evidence ink, focus-reveals-source, strict visible grid, tabular figures.

FINISH: unreviewed and unverified until the finish review passes.

Signature: a per-answer guilloché provenance mark. Petals are the rules cited and rings are the tool calls. It engraves while the answer is pending, and clicking it focuses that answer's evidence.
